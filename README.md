# LegalEase AI

## AI-Powered Legal Document Understanding & Assistance

> **Understand. Compare. Review. Prepare.**
>
> LegalEase AI helps employees understand complex legal documents, identify important information, compare document versions, organize potential areas for review, and prepare for conversations with qualified legal professionals.

---

## 🏆 Hackathon Theme

**AI for Legal Assistance & Access**

Legal information can often be complex, difficult to understand, and challenging to navigate without professional assistance.

LegalEase AI uses Generative AI to make document-based legal information more accessible through a structured, evidence-grounded workflow rather than a generic chatbot experience.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Employees may receive documents such as:

- Employment agreements
- Offer letters
- HR notices
- Policy documents
- Compensation-related documents
- Workplace agreements
- Other employment-related legal documents

These documents can contain complex terminology, obligations, deadlines, financial conditions, referenced policies, and clauses that are difficult to understand without professional assistance.

A generic AI chatbot can provide an answer, but users may still struggle to:

- Understand the complete document
- Identify important clauses
- Track dates and deadlines
- Understand responsibilities
- Compare document versions
- Identify information that requires further review
- Determine what information is missing
- Prepare questions for a legal professional
- Organize relevant information before a consultation

LegalEase AI addresses this gap through a structured document-understanding workflow.

---

# 💡 Solution

LegalEase AI transforms a complex legal document into an organized, document-grounded information workspace.

Instead of simply providing a chatbot response, the application guides the user through:

```text
Upload Document
       ↓
Understand Document
       ↓
Identify Important Clauses & Obligations
       ↓
Extract Dates & Deadlines
       ↓
Build Document-Grounded Timeline
       ↓
Identify Potential Areas for Review
       ↓
Review Actionable Information
       ↓
Prepare for a Legal Professional
       ↓
